Welcome to my homepage! I am a researcher in mathematical and theoretical physics, with a primary focus on the mathematical structures underlying quantum theory—including quantum field theory, quantum information, and quantum computation. My research interests lie in topological field theory, topological order, and generalized symmetries in both quantum field theory and lattice models. I am particularly drawn to axiomatic approaches to quantum field theory, such as the geometric and operator-algebraic frameworks. Along this path, I developed a deep interest in quantum algebra and topology, including tensor categories, (weak, quasi) Hopf algebras, quantum cohomology, TQFT, cobordism, low-dimensional topology, knots, braids, and related structures. I also work on quantum information theory and quantum computation, which is the field where I first began my research career. For more details, please refer to my research page and publication/preprint page.
