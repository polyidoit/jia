Academic Life

...

I enjoy almost all branches of mathematics and science and am eager to learn more. However, I am most familiar with quantum field theory, quantum information and quantum computation theory, machine learning, and various areas of mathematics, such as quantum algebra, quantum topology, and functional analysis, all of which I use in my research.

Maybe less is more, and vice versa. But life is our oyster—let's try more.

Beyond Academia

...

I enjoy visiting different countries and exploring the cultures there. I also like reading books, watching movies, and more. I have made a list of the books I’ve read and the movies I’ve watched available on Douban (ID: blackwormhole) (note: this is in Chinese, and login is required).

Museums, galleries and coffee shops are places where I like to spend my leisure time. I also enjoy hiking, and though I wish I were younger to explore more, I am getting older. More or less, my interest in exploring the world is fading.
