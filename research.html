Main Research Topics

A list of my current research directions.

  • Topological Order, TQFT and Related Mathematical Physics

    Generalized symmetry, Quantum double model, Levin-Wen string-net model, Chern-Simons theory, Quantum Hall effect, SPT and SET phases, etc.

    Coming soon...

  • Open System Topological Phase

    Open system dynamics (with non-Hermitian Hamiltonians as a special case), symmetry of Lindbladians, mixed-state long-range entanglement, mixed-state TQFT, etc.

    Coming soon...

  • Spatial and Temporol Quantum Correlations

    Bell-Kochen-Specker theorem, entanglement, quantum steering, Leggett-Garg inequality, pseudo-density operator, quantum process matrix, quantum causality, uncertainty relation, etc.

    Coming soon...

  • Tensor Network & Neural Network

    Efficient methods for simulating complex many-body quantum systems, quantum neural network states, neural network density operator, etc.

    Coming soon...

  • Quantum Machine Learning

    Various quantum neural networks, the no free lunch theorem, trainability, etc.

Research Interests

My research focuses on quantum information and quantum computation, topological order and TQFT, quantum topology and algebra, etc.

TQFT, CFT, tensor categories, Hopf algebras, weak Hopf algebras, quasi-Hopf algebras, Frobenius algebras.

Topological order, SPT, SET, mixed-state topological phases.

Generalized symmetry, non-invertible symmetry, higher-form symmetry, subsystem symmetry.

Topological quantum error-correcting codes, homological codes, topological quantum computation.

Space-time states, pseudo-density operators, superdensity operators, doubled density operators.

Quantum causality, process matrices, quantum Bayesian inference, quantum retrodiction.

Quantum correlations: quantum discord, quantum entanglement, quantum steering, Bell nonlocality, quantum contextuality.
